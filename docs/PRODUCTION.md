# From prototype to a real service

This is a development boundary document, not a claim of regulatory clearance or legal advice.

1. **Clinical governance:** emergency physicians must own intended use, fixed protocols, translations, escalation paths, assessment of false negatives/positives, prospective validation, and adverse-event review. OpenCV image quality is not medical assessment. Assess applicable CDSCO medical-device/software requirements before clinical use.
2. **Hospital participation:** signed agreements, verified capabilities and staffed capacity, response deadlines, acceptance ownership, freshness thresholds and escalation. Emergency stabilisation and transfers remain clinical decisions; reservations must not override clinical priority.
3. **Emergency integration:** authorised 112/ambulance interfaces and dispatch operations; acknowledgement, delivery and failure receipts. An incident notification is not an automatically registered FIR. Preserve Good Samaritan rights.
4. **Procurement:** authorised buyers/suppliers, funding, purchase approval, lead times, receiving checks, recalls, storage/cold-chain records and contracts governing returns/replacement.
5. **Disposal:** authorised collection and treatment partners, chain of custody, verified certificates and applicable biomedical-waste requirements. A button click cannot prove disposal occurred.
6. **Tariffs and claims:** applicable scheme and agreement eligibility, official rate sources, package inclusions, noncovered items, taxes, exceptions, disputes, fraud controls and independent audit. NPPA medicine ceilings and scheme package rates are not a universal state price list for every private service.
7. **Payments:** an identified funded payer, verified hospital beneficiaries, authorised payment infrastructure, reconciliation, failed-transfer recovery and scheduler. No escrow/custody of real funds is implemented here.
8. **Privacy and security:** applicable Indian data-protection requirements, lawful processing bases, consent where appropriate, emergency access rules, verified identities, facility-scoped permissions, encryption, retention, access logging, incident response and independent security assessment. Remove freely selectable demo roles before handling real records.
9. **Reliability:** cloud database transactions, facility-level resource locks, idempotency keys, outbox/event delivery, monitoring, backups, disaster recovery, capacity-data staleness, robust offline synchronisation and 24/7 operations.
10. **Validation:** measured time to acknowledgement, conflicting capacity commitments, handover completeness, staff workload, usability with stressed users, accessibility, and failure recovery. Run simulated exercises before any authorised clinical pilot.
